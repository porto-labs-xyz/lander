# Kiln Monument release

The current approved site replaces the PR's older grille-animation design. It uses Monument Grotesk Regular/Medium/Bold webfonts, the “Keep the work close” framing, a close-up One introduction, native One/Atelier/Rack carousel, square-only active tabs, concise privacy/signup copy and individual product pages. Animated grille panels are not mounted.

The founder's final refinement replaces the lower One close-up with a black Atelier on a near-black background. This is a concept colour treatment, not a shipping finish announcement. The image is lazy-loaded, 1536 × 1024 WebP (43 KB), with live HTML copy and responsive cropping.

## Connections

- Registration and contact both POST to the existing Formspree form `https://formspree.io/f/xjgepgbg`.
- Registration requires the explicit product/launch email opt-in and sends the selected hardware interest, consent text and privacy version. Contact sends its reason/message without opting into marketing.
- Success appears only after a successful, readable provider response without errors. Failure or timeout preserves the form; pending requests prevent duplicates; navigation aborts pending work.
- Contact offers “Book a conversation” at `/book/`. That page preserves the original Google Calendar appointment embed and direct fallback URL. Browser QA confirmed the “30 min with Richard & Peter” schedule and available appointment times.
- Website privacy describes Formspree and Google Calendar. Unsubscribe/contact requests can be sent to the existing public team address.
